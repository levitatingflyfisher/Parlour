# Privacy

Parlour collects nothing.

- **No accounts, no sign-in.** There is no server to sign in to.
- **No network calls.** After the page loads, Parlour never talks to the
  internet. Every game runs entirely on your device, which is why it works
  offline.
- **No ads, no analytics, no trackers.** None. Ever.
- **Games are never saved.** Leaving a game, reloading or closing the page
  ends it. No scores, no history, no streaks.
- **One setting is stored.** If you pick Light or Dark, Parlour remembers that
  one word in this browser under the name `parlour.theme`, so the page opens
  the way you left it. Picking Auto (follow your device) deletes it. It never
  leaves the device, and clearing your browser data clears it.
- **An offline copy of the app.** Your browser keeps a copy of Parlour itself
  (the page and its icon) so it works without a connection. That copy holds
  nothing about you.

It's a games cupboard, not a data business.
