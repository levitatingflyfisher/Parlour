A cupboard of calm family games that respect you.

Offline, no accounts, no ads, no engagement traps — and every game is a tiny, unit-tested module you could read end to end if you wanted to.

• Pass-and-play and versus-the-computer
• Games that end when you stop, not when a timer says so
• No leaderboards, no streaks, no notifications begging you back

Nothing here is trying to keep you on your phone.

Free and open source, MIT licensed.
